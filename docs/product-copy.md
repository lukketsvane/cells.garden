# Product descriptions

## Short description

Turn projects and tasks into a pixel-art garden. Plan, work and watch it grow.

## Web app

Give your projects a place to grow. Each project is a plant, each task is a cell, and every step forward changes your garden.

Keep ideas in Minerals, prepare the groundwork in Roots, move work into Stem and celebrate what is finished in Flowers. Switch between the board and the garden whenever you want a different view.

Start on this device without an account. Sign in to sync across devices, share a garden and assign cells to friends. Free and open source.

## Obsidian plugin

Grow a task garden inside your vault. Open cells.garden in an Obsidian tab, plant a project and arrange its cells as ideas, groundwork, work in progress and finished results.

Your garden stays in the plugin's data file. An optional account syncs it with the web app and Chrome extension. Your notes stay untouched unless you explicitly import an existing Garden-Cells folder.

## Chrome extension

A small garden for the work ahead. Open your task board in a new tab, keep it beside a page in the side panel, or glance at a plant from the toolbar. Changes stay in sync across the extension. Sign in to share the same garden with the web app and Obsidian.

## Pin mineral concept

The working sketch is at `/previews/pin-mineral.html`. A single selected mineral opens a panel in the void, with plain notes, bullets and interactive checkboxes. Switching minerals replaces the panel; closing it returns attention to the garden. Its sample data is separate from real gardens.
