GuideSync and sharing

Sync your garden. Share only what you mean to.

Use one garden across devices, or work with someone on a shared project. Sync and collaboration are optional; local use does not require an account.

Local use and account sync are different

Without signing in, your garden belongs to that browser, extension installation or Obsidian plugin data file. Opening cells.garden somewhere else does not automatically show that local garden.

To sync, sign in with the same account everywhere you want to work: the web app, Chrome extension, Obsidian plugin or Home Screen app. Use an internet connection for account access and syncing changes.

Before moving an existing local garden or changing accounts, download a backup. A backup gives you a copy you can inspect and import if you need it.

Share one plant or an entire garden

  • One plant: use the share control on its project card. The invited person gets that plant in their own garden and can edit it with you.
  • A garden: use Share garden in the garden menu to invite someone into the garden you own.
  • A separate workspace: choose New garden space when you want a distinct group of projects to share.

Share invite links privately. Anyone with an active invite link can join and edit the associated garden or plant after signing in. These links are not read-only public portfolios.

Assign a task

  1. Share the garden or plant with the person who will do the work.
  2. Right-click a cell, or press and hold on a touch screen.
  3. Choose Assign and select the collaborator.

Their avatar appears beside the task, and the assignment appears in their notification inbox. Optional phone alerts are separate; see the mobile notification setup.

Manage invitation access

The sharing panel lets you create a New link, turn a link off and manage people who already joined. Making a new link invalidates the old invitation.

Turning off an invitation stops new people using that link; it does not remove existing members. Use the people list to remove a member. Do not post private invitation links in search-indexed pages, screenshots or public issue reports.

If a garden looks different on another device

  1. Confirm both places are signed in to the same account.
  2. Check you have opened the same garden space, not a different shared garden.
  3. Check the network connection and wait for sync before making another copy.
  4. Keep a backup before importing anything. Merge updates matching plants, while Replace discards the open garden’s current contents.

If the problem remains, report it with the affected platform and steps, without passwords, invite links or private project text.
