Your project garden on your phone

Open cells.garden from your Home Screen, with your projects and task board in one place. You can start locally without an account.

Open cells.garden

Install on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open cells.garden in Safari.
  2. Open Safari’s Share menu. Depending on your layout, it is available directly or inside the page menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If shown, enable Open as Web App, then confirm with Add.
  4. Open the new garden icon from your Home Screen.

If the option is missing, check Safari’s available actions. Apple’s web-app installation guide shows the current steps.

Install on Android

  1. Open cells.garden in Chrome.
  2. Open the menu beside the address bar and choose Install and create shortcut → Install. The wording may differ between browser versions.
  3. Follow the confirmation and open the garden from your launcher or Home Screen.

See Google’s Android web-app instructions if your browser menu looks different.

What works offline?

Let the garden finish loading while online first. The app caches its interface so it can reopen offline. You can work with the garden stored on that device; signing in, receiving shared changes and syncing between devices require a connection.

Local storage is not a backup. Before clearing browser data or uninstalling, download a copy of your garden. Avoid private browsing for a garden you want to keep.

See the same projects on another device

Sign in with the same account on your phone and in the web app, Chrome extension or Obsidian plugin. Installing the app alone does not connect separate local gardens. Read how sync and sharing work.

Optional assignment notifications

To receive assignment alerts, sign in, open Settings → Notifications and choose Turn on. Allow notifications when your browser asks. On iPhone and iPad, use the Home Screen app on iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later.

Notifications are optional and depend on browser support and device settings. Assignment text may appear on your lock screen. You can turn them off in the app’s settings. The Obsidian plugin and Chrome extension have an in-app inbox; phone push alerts are enabled separately in the web app.
