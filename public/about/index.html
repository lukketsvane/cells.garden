Give your projects a place to grow

cells.garden is a project board and a pixel-art garden. Each project is a plant, and its cells hold your tasks, plans, ideas and progress.

Use it on the web, in Chrome’s new tab, side panel or toolbar popup, and inside Obsidian. Start locally without an account, or sign in to sync and collaborate.

Version 0.2.0 is our beta milestone across the three platforms. The garden is still growing: feedback and bug reports help shape what comes next.

Questions and feedback

Open a GitHub issue or email cells.garden@proton.me. In the app, open the garden menu and choose Report issue for both options.

Please keep passwords, sign-in links and private garden data out of public issues.

Your work stays yours

Work offline without an account. Optional sync stores your garden with the cells.garden service; shared members can see and edit what you share. Export a backup from the garden menu, and read the privacy policy for storage and account details.
