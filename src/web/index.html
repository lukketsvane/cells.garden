Cells Garden: a visual project planner

Turn projects and tasks into a pixel-art garden. Each project is a plant, each task is a cell, and your garden changes as work moves forward.

Organize ideas, plans, work in progress and finished tasks on a kanban board. Start on this device without an account, or sign in to sync and share your garden.

Free and open source. Available on the web, in Chrome and in Obsidian.
